## App Store — English

**Name (30):** `MemDoping: Memory Games`
**Subtitle (30):** `Play, learn, remember`
**Promotional text (170):**
> Turn learning into the game. Short, science-based memory missions you can
> finish in a few minutes — then pick up right where you left off.

**Keywords (100, comma-separated, no spaces):**
`memory,memory games,brain training,memory training,recall,focus,mnemonic,memory palace,learn,study,concentration`

**Description (≤4000):**
> **Don't add games to learning — make learning the game.**
>
> MemDoping turns science-based memory techniques into short, playful missions.
> Each one is a few minutes: pick it up, learn through play, recall what stuck,
> and level up. Stop any time — your progress is saved.
>
> **Learn real techniques, as games**
> • Attention & Encoding — decide something about each item so it sticks
> • Chunking — break a long number into small groups you can hold
> • Retrieval Practice — spell answers from memory, not from a list
> • Association & Imagery — build a vivid scene for each pair
> • Interleaving — mix categories so you learn to tell them apart
> • Elaboration — ask "why" to tie facts to what you know
> • Story Linking — chain items into one story to recall the order
> • Method of Loci — walk a memory palace and leave items along the way
>
> **Built to help you actually remember**
> • Spaced review brings items back over days, when you're about to forget
> • A Memory Score and Retention indicator track your recall over time
> • Difficulty gently adapts to you
> • Night Doping: a calm, untimed wind-down mode
>
> **Comfortable by design**
> • Reduce Motion, sound and haptic controls
> • Clear stopping points — no pressure, no punishing streaks
>
> Regular use may support memory performance, focus, and recall. Results vary by
> person; there is no 100% improvement guarantee. MemDoping is a learning game,
> not a medical or diagnostic tool.
